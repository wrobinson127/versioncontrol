What each lab published

Each release splits into four cells: system card, model card, usage policy and paper. A filled square is a link on record; an empty square, we checked and found none; a short dash, not yet researched.

"Not disclosed" is a fact about the lab. "Not yet researched" is work we have not done.

Fewer arrive with a paper. In 2023, 24 of 34 researched releases had a paper on record (71%). In 2026 so far, 9 of 42 (21%). Papers can come out weeks after a release, so recent years may rise. In 2026, 15 of the 42 checked releases are from September or later, a period that holds 21 of the year's 93 releases. The checked releases lean toward the newest ones, so the share may move as earlier 2026 releases are checked.

What the lab said, beside what Epoch measured

Each row is one release scored twice on one benchmark: the open diamond is the lab's own number, tagged LAB; the filled dot is Epoch's measurement, tagged IND.

5 releases were graded twice on the same benchmark: once by the releasing lab, once by Epoch. 2 of the 6 pairs sit within a single point, and the gaps run both ways. In three of the six pairs (Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite, GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.5) the lab and Epoch ran different reasoning settings, so those gaps include a settings difference. These six pairs come from recent major releases, and the lab and Epoch often run different settings (reasoning effort, number of attempts). With so few pairs this is not a test of whether labs overstate. The record keeps both numbers and never averages them.

What the record shows, in four findings

Independent scores rose. Fewer releases came with a paper or a parameter count on record. More came with a system card.

From 2023 to 2026, the highest independent GPQA Diamond score rose from 36 to 95. The share of researched releases with a paper fell from 71% to 21%, and the share with a parameter count in Epoch AI's data fell from 71% to 40%. The share with a system card rose from 11% to 48% (2 of 18, then 19 of 40). In 2026, 15 of the 42 checked releases are from September or later, a period that holds 21 of the year's 93 releases. The checked releases lean toward the newest ones, so these 2026 shares may move as earlier 2026 releases are checked.
