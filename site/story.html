The story of the record

The record begins

On 2022-11-30, OpenAI released ChatGPT, and the release of a large AI model became a public event. This site is the record of what shipped after that day.

It is a registry, not a review. A pipeline watches four independent signals nightly; a person approves every entry before it appears. Each entry states what shipped, from whom, how it can be used, and what the lab disclosed.

Every number in the record carries a tag naming where it came from. That rule is the whole design. The rest of this page shows you how to read it.

The pulse builds

Each mark is one release from one of 12 tracked organizations. The record holds 281 of them, from 2022-12-22 to 2026-06-30.

The spacing is the story the record tells on its own. In the record's first full year, the median gap between releases across all tracked organizations was 4 days.

This year it is 3 days. The counter beside the strip is computed from the release dates you are looking at; nothing else feeds it.

The climb

The record pins two benchmarks: GPQA Diamond and SWE-Bench Verified. Each point on this chart is one model's score, placed at its release date.

A solid mark is an independent measurement, run by an evaluator that is not the releasing lab. In v1 of the record, these come from Epoch AI's Benchmarking Hub.

A hollow mark is a number the releasing lab reported for its own model. The record keeps both, and never lets one dress as the other. The tag travels with the number at every size, on every surface.

The treadmill

Benchmarks age. Scores crowd a ceiling, the test stops separating models, and the field moves to a harder ruler.

When that happens, the record does not stitch the old ruler to the new one. The era closes with a labeled boundary, and the axis that follows is a different axis.

"GPQA Diamond" is permanently a different measurement from "GPQA." A number on one never compares to a number on the other, here or anywhere in the record. Where a comparison cannot be made honestly, the record says so, in words.

Two numbers

One model, one benchmark, two numbers: the one the lab reported, and the one an independent evaluator measured. They are often different. Neither is deleted.

Every number tells you where it came from, or it does not appear.

The paperwork

Models ship with paperwork, or without it: a system card, a model card, a usage policy, a paper. The record tracks each artifact for each release, as a link.

Where a lab published nothing, the cell stays hollow, and it stays hollow on purpose. "Not disclosed" is recorded signal, not missing data. It is never rendered as a zero.

The landing

This is the record. It re-checks its sources nightly, drafts candidate entries, and waits for a person to approve them; nothing publishes on its own.

Everything above is built from the same files you can download below: the entries, the derived comparisons, and a manifest with checksums. The methodology page states the rules; the data page carries the record itself. From here, it is yours to search.
