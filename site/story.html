What the lab said, beside what Epoch measured

5 releases were graded twice on the same benchmark: once by the releasing lab, once by Epoch. 2 of the 6 pairs sit within a single point, and the gaps run both ways. In three of the six pairs (Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite, GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.5) the lab and Epoch ran different reasoning settings, so those gaps include a settings difference. These six pairs come from recent major releases, and the lab and Epoch often run different settings (reasoning effort, number of attempts). With so few pairs this is not a test of whether labs overstate. The record keeps both numbers and never averages them.

Fewer arrive with a paper

In 2023, 24 of 34 researched releases had a paper on record (71%). In 2026 so far, 9 of 42 (21%). Papers can come out weeks after a release, so recent years may rise. In 2026, 15 of the 42 checked releases are from September or later, a period that holds 21 of the year's 93 releases. The checked releases lean toward the newest ones, so the share may move as earlier 2026 releases are checked.

And fewer have a parameter count on record

In 2023, 32 of 45 releases had a parameter count in Epoch AI's data (71%). In 2026 so far, 37 of 93 (40%). Epoch often adds counts for recent models late, and we have not checked the blanks against the labs' own pages.

What changed, 2023 to 2026

From 2023 to 2026, the highest independent GPQA Diamond score rose from 36 to 95. The share of researched releases with a paper fell from 71% to 21%, and the share with a parameter count in Epoch AI's data fell from 71% to 40%. The share with a system card rose from 11% to 48% (2 of 18, then 19 of 40). In 2026, 15 of the 42 checked releases are from September or later, a period that holds 21 of the year's 93 releases. The checked releases lean toward the newest ones, so these 2026 shares may move as earlier 2026 releases are checked.

What the record shows, in four findings

  • Cadence45 releases in 2023, 120 in 2025. Pooled across the twelve labs, the median time between one release and the next was 4 days in 2023, 2.5 in 2024 and 2 in 2025.
  • Independent scoresThe highest independent GPQA Diamond score went from 36 to 95; SWE-Bench Verified from 31 to 84. Every figure measured by Epoch.
  • Lab beside independent5 releases were graded both ways, giving 6 pairs; 2 fall within a single point. That is too few pairs, run at different settings, to test whether labs overstate. Both numbers are kept, neither is averaged.

The record is public. Each night it checks its sources and drafts new entries, and a person approves each one before it appears. Every number shows where it came from, or it is left out.
