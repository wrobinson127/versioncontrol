Releases nearly tripled from 2023 to 2025

Pooled across the twelve labs, the median wait between one release and the next fell from 3.5 days in 2023 to 2 in 2025.

Revisions, updates to a model already on record, are left out of these counts: 14 so far.

Five of the twelve labs have no system card on any checked release

Model cards: 29 of 45 checked releases in 2023 (64%), 50 of 93 in 2026 so far (54%). Usage policies: 38 of 45 (84%), then 94 of 94 (100%).

The highest independent score on GPQA Diamond (graduate-level science questions) rose from 36 in 2023 to 95 in 2026

On a second benchmark, SWE-bench Verified (fixing real GitHub issues), Epoch's top score rose from 31 in 2024 to 84 in 2026.

The highest open-weights GPQA Diamond score on record is 91.9 (GLM-5.2, 2026).

The lab's score is above Epoch's in five of six pairs

In three (Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite, GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.5) the lab ran a higher reasoning setting than Epoch, so those gaps include a settings difference.

Six pairs from recent releases cannot test whether labs overstate.

The wait between releases shortened at eleven of the twelve labs

For example, median days between releases at first and lately: OpenAI 66 to 22, Anthropic 104 to 21, Google DeepMind 41 to 15, Alibaba (Qwen) 20.5 to 11.5. "At first" is the median of a lab's first third of gaps on record; "lately", of its last third.

ByteDance, Tencent and Baidu are not tracked, so the record cannot support a comparison between countries.

What the lab said, beside what Epoch measured

5 releases were graded twice on the same benchmark: once by the releasing lab, once by Epoch. 2 of the 6 pairs sit within a single point. In three (Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite, GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.5) the lab ran a higher reasoning setting than Epoch, so those gaps include a settings difference. With so few pairs this is not a test of whether labs overstate. Both numbers are kept; neither is averaged.

Fewer arrive with a paper

In 2023, 27 of 45 checked releases had a paper on record (60%). In 2026 so far, 22 of 92 (24%). Papers can come out weeks after a release, so recent years may rise.

And fewer have a parameter count on record

In 2023, 26 of 45 releases had a parameter count on record (58%). In 2026 so far, 34 of 94 (36%). Counts reported by others are left out. Epoch often adds counts for recent models late, and we have not checked the blanks against the labs' own pages.

What changed, 2023 to 2026

From 2023 to 2026, the highest independent GPQA Diamond score rose from 36 to 95. The share of checked releases with a paper fell from 60% to 24%, and the share with a parameter count on record fell from 58% to 36%. The share with a system card rose from 11% to 35% (5 of 45, then 32 of 92).

From 2023 to 2026, fewer releases came with a paper and more with a system card

Papers can come out weeks after a release, so recent years may rise. Parameter counts are Epoch AI's and divide by all releases, leaving out counts reported by others; Epoch often adds counts for recent models late, and we have not checked the blanks against the labs' own pages.
