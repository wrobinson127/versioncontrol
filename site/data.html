The dataset

The complete registry record is published as versioned files: a manifest, one gzipped JSON array of entries per year, and a derived deltas file. The same files drive this site; there is no separate API and no private copy.

Files

FileContents
manifest.jsonIndex of entry files with per-file entry counts, ids, and SHA-256 checksums.
entries/2022.json.gzEntries dated 2022.
entries/2023.json.gzEntries dated 2023.

derived/site_meta.jsonRecord counts, date range, and the last successful update timestamp.

What the dataset does not contain

Pricing and context-length figures displayed on the site with a "per OpenRouter" attribution are display-only and are not included in the downloadable dataset, because OpenRouter publishes no explicit redistribution license for its catalog data.

Cost of operation

The registry runs on static hosting and scheduled jobs at zero infrastructure cost, plus a small metered LLM cost for the nightly drafting step, expected to stay under one dollar per month. Costs are stated here so the record's independence is checkable.
