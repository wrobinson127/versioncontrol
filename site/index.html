Before this record begins

This record begins on 2022-11-30, the day OpenAI released ChatGPT and the release cadence of large AI models became a public event. Models existed before that date. GPT-3 shipped as an API in 2020; instruction-tuned variants, open research models, and commercial systems preceded and accompanied it. The registry does not attempt to reconstruct that earlier history: disclosure practices, benchmark conventions, and release mechanics before late 2022 differ enough from what followed that entries built from today's sources would misrepresent them. For the earlier record, Epoch AI's "Data on AI Models" dataset reaches back decades and documents its own methods.
