How to read a star

  • Larger and lit: an independent score is on record.
  • Amber ring: the lab reported its own score.
  • Halo, clockwise from the top: system card, model card, usage policy, paper. Solid is on record, dashed is not disclosed, a dot is not yet researched.
  • Small hollow ring: a revision of a model already on record.

The charts and figures on this page are drawn from the record with JavaScript. The full record is also on the timeline and in the dataset files.
