## Required attribution for Epoch AI derived fields

Portions of this dataset are derived from Epoch AI's "Data on AI Models," used under CC-BY 4.0. If you redistribute or build on those fields, you must retain the following attribution:

> Epoch AI, "Data on AI Models". Published online at epoch.ai. Retrieved from https://epoch.ai/data/ai-models [online resource].

(The exact citation format is published on Epoch AI's documentation page; this block is verified against it during the project's source-verification pass.)

## Fields derived from Epoch AI

The dataset manifest identifies which fields on which entries are Epoch-derived. In general: backfilled release dates, organization mappings, parameter counts, accessibility classes, and independent benchmark numbers sourced from Epoch's Benchmarking Hub carry Epoch attribution.

## What the published dataset does not contain

Pricing and context-length figures displayed on the site with a "per OpenRouter" attribution are display-only and are not included in the downloadable dataset, because OpenRouter publishes no explicit redistribution license for its catalog data.
