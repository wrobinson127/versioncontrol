## Fields derived from Epoch AI

Release dates, lab names, parameter counts and availability (Epoch's "Model accessibility" field) come from Epoch AI's models dataset, "Data on AI Models". Every independent benchmark score comes from Epoch AI's "Capabilities & benchmarking" data (earlier called the AI Benchmarking Hub). Each entry's `sources` and each score's `provenance` block name the source and its retrieval date.

## What the published dataset does not contain

OpenRouter's catalog is not in the dataset, and the site does not display its prices or context lengths. Prices quoted in an entry's summary are the lab's own figures. Artificial Analysis data is not in the dataset either; it is used only for an internal coverage check.
